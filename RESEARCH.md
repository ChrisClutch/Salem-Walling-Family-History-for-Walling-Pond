An alternate George Washington Walling is identified as Gabriel's **cousin** by West Linn Historical Society. Do not silently turn George into Gabriel's son, even though another biography appears to describe a “son George”; this is a conflict to resolve with primary genealogical records.

## Editorial/ethical guardrails

- Distinguish all historic claims from direct record images and state a source access date.
- Do not present a draft registrant as a veteran.
- Do not infer genealogy from surname or an adjacent grave alone.
- No contact data, private details or unverified allegations about living individuals.
- No implication that the family has endorsed housing, public acquisition or site access.
- Do not infer the entire 9.8 acres is developable; pond area, zoning and reclamation matter.
- No fabricated historic imagery, quotations or design approvals.

## Proposed concept for website and owner discussion (2026-10-25)

**Walling Veterans Cottages:** small, accessible homes clustered around a landscaped common green, with a community room, memorial garden and carefully managed views or access to the existing pond where feasible. This is a **proposal from the project sponsor, not an existing project or a Walling family commitment**. First technical gate is whether the industrial-zoned former quarry has sufficient safe buildable upland and a viable land-use path. No numerical unit count is claimed.

**Methodological limit:** The web accessible research here is a strong discovery pass across historical societies, institutional archival finding aids, transcribed census/cemetery sources, military extracts, 2026 press, and current brokerage. It does **not** substitute for paid databases, physical archive boxes, original military service packets, land title and a full generation-by-generation proof argument. An exhaustive list of all descendants who served cannot honestly be asserted from the records inspected.
